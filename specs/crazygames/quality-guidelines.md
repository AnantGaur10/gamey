# CrazyGames — Quality Guidelines (recommended)

Source: https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/quality
Non-mandatory but drives featuring/retention. Subjective but battle-tested.

## Onboarding

- Land in gameplay. In-gameplay tutorial, core only, skippable.
- Visuals > text. Show keyboard overlay / mouse gestures.
- Clear UI: labeled buttons, no ad-bait sizing, no delays to confuse.

## General principles

- Clear reachable goals. Easy to learn/understand (correct language or universal icons). Consistent intuitive controls.

## Fun

- Responsive, balanced challenge/strategy/story pacing, comfortable layout, appropriate audio, device-appropriate UI, fun for segments, interesting scenarios, no boring grind, snappy flow, solo as prominent as friends-mode (or explain friends-only).

## Uniqueness

- Extensible (new levels/art/story). Don't change genre post-submit. Maintain/update frequently. Distinct name/icon (not confused with other game, no чуж IP).

## Polish

- Graphics: high-res, consistent, no compression artifacts, coherent style (don't mix realistic/cartoon, hi/lo res). Name/imagery truthful to genre.
- Audio: consistent levels, not too loud/quiet, music fits visuals.

## Restricted keys

- Layout-agnostic bindings (AZERTY `ZQSD` vs `WASD` — France).
- Avoid web-reserved: `Esc` closes fullscreen, `Ctrl/Cmd+W` closes tab (can only suppress in fullscreen).
