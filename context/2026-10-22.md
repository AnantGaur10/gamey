# 2026-10-22

## 1. Store covers + metadata (user picked "Store metadata + covers" from the open backlog)

- Spec: `specs/crazygames/game-covers.md` (3 covers 1920x1080 / 800x1200 /
  800x800; name on the cover in a stylized font; hero / artistic hook,
  not a raw screenshot; no borders, extra text, store icons).
- `npm run build:covers` = `scripts/blender/build_covers.py` (Cycles CPU,
  128 samples + denoise, `--factory-startup`, reads only
  `public/models/*.glb`, never a .blend) -> text-free plates in
  `store/covers/plates/` (gitignored), then `scripts/covers.mjs` (headless
  Chromium) lays the title over them -> `store/covers/cover_<w>x<h>.png`.
  `store/` sits outside `public/`, so none of it ships in `dist/`.
- Scene: TELEGRAPH ROW (`town.glb`) at sunset. Main Street was tried first,
  but its facades are a solid wall behind the foe, so the foe never reads
  against the sky. On Telegraph Row the open far end of the street,
  the poles and the wires frame a low sun, and the foe (outlaw = Black
  Bart, ladder level 2) stands right in front of the sun with a long shadow
  toward the camera. The hero is seen from behind his right hip, low camera
  (0.6m, level, lens shift up for sky), in the game's idle pose (hand on the
  holstered grip, gun moved into the `{P}_GunHolster` socket like
  `makeGunHolster().holster()`).
- Cowboys are centred on the pelvis with their feet on the ground, like
  `cowboyGlb.ts`. Outfit GLBs are authored off to the side (x 3.2), so
  placing their raw root put the foe ~2.5m off.
- Look: vertex colour (baked palette x AO) as base colour + 0.12 emission,
  a warm sun lamp (elevation ~8.5 deg) and a soft warm fill for non-camera
  rays. The camera sees the game's EVENING sky gradient (`arena.ts`) with
  a sun disc + glow. Dusk haze in the compositor comes from the Z pass
  (8 -> 70m, up to 45%, never on the sky). Standard view transform (AgX
  washed the palette out).
- Title: HIGH NOON over DUEL, the home screen's h1 (`.menu.home h1`): Rye,
  the gold gradient, a dark drop shadow, DUEL letter-spaced between gold
  rules, auto-fit to 50% / 88% / 78% of the width, top centre (over the
  sky).
- Look-dev: `COVER_SCALE=50 COVER_SAMPLES=32 COVER_ONLY=land|port|square
  COVER_OUT=<dir>` (~25s per frame at 50%); `COVER_SET`, `COVER_FOE`
  swap the map / outfit.
- `store/metadata.md`: name, tags, short + long description, desktop and
  touch controls, portal notes. Each claim cites the code it comes from;
  strip the brackets when pasting.
- Still open: preview videos (15-20s, 1080p landscape + portrait, first frame
  = cover), music + hit SFX.
