## 5. Focus becomes a timing QTE + holster-leave restart (user ask; overrides 09-26 §1 tap/mash input)
- User: "instead of smashing spacebar/enter ... a small quick time event ... a moving vertical line in a rectangle stops exactly in a highlighted portion ... as many times as possible in the given period before DRAW" and "if the player leaves the holster before [DRAW] the whole original countdown period starts again".
- Logic: `src/game/timingQte.ts` (pure, seeded from the duel seed). Needle ping-pongs 0..1; traverse 0.48s, x0.93 per hit (floor 0.3s). Gold zone 22% of the bar; centre 30% of the zone = PERFECT. A press freezes the needle 0.12s where it stopped (result readable, presses ignored meanwhile).
- Follow-up (user, same day): EVERY press moves the zone; a hit narrows it (x0.86, floor 8%) and speeds the needle, a miss widens it (x1.18, cap 32%) and resets the streak. New zones spawn 0.22-0.55 AHEAD of the needle along its travel (bounces included) so attempts come fast: a perfect player fits 7-9 hits in the 3s window (pure-Node sim, 5 seeds), vs ~4-5 before.
- Bloom: `DuelMachine.addQte` — perfect shrinks `focusPerTapDeg x 1.2` (0.36°), good `x 0.8` (0.24°), miss = old `addMiss` (+0.25°). ~5 perfect hits reach `bloomMin` (more attempts fit now). Tunable, not locked.
- Clock: advanced only in the fixed-step callback while Focus runs (not paused); rendered and JUDGED at `peek(alpha*STEP + wall time since that frame)`, so what the player sees is what gets judged at any refresh rate. `test:physics` now checks the rendered needle sits on the fixed-clock curve at 30/60/144/165/240Hz.
- Input: PC Space/Enter (key repeat ignored, so holding does nothing); a mouse click during Focus is still a miss. Touch: the old two tap pads are gone; any tap during Focus is a press.
- Holster (PC): leaving the zone during Focus calls `machine.resetFocus()` + `qte.reset()` (3.0s countdown, start bloom, zone/speed/streak) and pauses; returning starts a fresh 3.0s. Bloom resets too, otherwise leaving at 0.1s would buy another 3s of QTE hits. Touch stays committed once started (no hover to track).
- UI: `.qte` bar docked bottom-left (where the keybar was): label, track with gold zone + cream perfect band + white needle, PERFECT/GOOD/MISS flash + `xN` streak, slim bloom-progress line. Cue reads `FOCUS! 2.3s` (was `TAP!`).
- Tests: specs/scripts wait for `FOCUS` (was `TAP`); `qteHits()` presses through the real window keydown only while the needle is inside the zone (DEV `__gamey.qte()` probe). New: QTE miss grows bloom and widens + moves the zone; leaving the holster restarts the countdown (desktop only). `ai-play.ts` uses timed hits, waits for the next round's `HOLSTER UP` cue (fixes the round-2 `boundingBox` race), logs any HTTP >= 400 URL.
