# 2026-10-26: phone pass (home layout, two-thumb touch controls)

User played on an Android phone over a Cloudflare quick tunnel (Chrome custom tab, landscape ~830x320 CSS, portrait ~400x780).

## 1. Home layout on phones
- "the play and name getting mixed": on a ~320px-tall landscape screen the bottom-anchored options stack climbed into the title; in portrait the top-right chips (name, gold, sound) sat on the title.
- Two more breakpoints in `style.css`: `max-height: 420px` (smaller title / options / chips) and `max-width: 600px and min-height: 421px` (portrait: title starts under the chips, `clamp()` font). Measured with no overlap at 830x320, 740x300, 860x380, 900x440, 800x450, 907x510, 412x780, 393x700, 360x640.

## 2. Two-thumb touch controls (locked, user)
- "make sure the people playing have to hold their thumb in the holster" / "one thumb is for holding the holster and the other is for handling the qte" / "left thumb on holster right thumb for qte" / "the mobile players can currently shoot wherever they tap and i want this to be removed the have to drag the crosshair where they want and shoot on a shoot button to shoot" / "there shouldn't be any crosshair until the holster phase is done".
- Focus on touch: the holster finger (`hold` in `runDuel`) must stay DOWN and IN the zone. Lifting it or sliding off pauses + resets Focus exactly like a mouse leaving the zone on PC (3.0s, bloom, QTE). A new touch on the zone re-engages. Replaces "touch is committed once started" (2026-10-05).
- The rule follows the input that started Focus (`focusByTouch`), not the device: a mouse on a touch device keeps the PC rule (the mobile-800 Playwright project drives the mouse).
- QTE on touch: only taps on the right-hand pad (`.tpad`, bottom right, size `clamp(h*0.26, 88, 180)`, the cylinder moves to its left) count; the QTE ring draws on the pad (the holster thumb would cover it). Off-pad taps do nothing.
- No crosshair on touch until DRAW.
- DRAW on touch: the pad becomes SHOOT (red round button). Aim = relative drag: the crosshair starts on the parked aim point and moves by the finger's travel x1.2 (`TOUCH_AIM_GAIN`), so it never sits under the thumb. The holster thumb carries on as the aim finger. Tap-to-fire and second-finger fire are gone.
- `touch-action: none` on everything in a duel (`body.in-duel #app *`): with `manipulation` a pan pointercancelled the held thumb / aiming drag.

## 3. Touch tutorial
- Coach cards on touch say LEFT THUMB / RIGHT THUMB (holster, focus, draw) + a new `lifted` card when the holster thumb comes up mid-Focus (back to the focus card on re-engage).
- Every touch device sees the tutorial once more, veterans included (user: "ye include veternas also"): `needsTutorial(adapter, p, touch)` checks the SafeStore device flag `gamey.touchTutorialSeen` (device, not account: the controls belong to the device); `markTutorialSeen` sets it.

## 4. Phone testing (DEV only)
- `vite.config.ts`: `server.host: "0.0.0.0"` + the tunnel host in `allowedHosts` (quick tunnels get a new random host per run).
- `src/main.ts`: in DEV a touch requests fullscreen (`navigationUI: "hide"`) + landscape lock, so the browser bar stays out of the way. Never in the build (CrazyGames: no custom fullscreen button; checked: not in `dist/`), never under webdriver, `?nofs` opts out.
