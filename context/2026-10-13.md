### Measuring first
- DEV probe `__gamey.ragdollLab({ wound, n, secs, head, shot, view })` (`home.ts`): n foe deaths from a wound pose on the live arm pose, each a throwaway `createRagdoll` hit along the duel line (deterministic jitter per trial index; `head` = headshot share) and stepped `secs` on the fixed 1/60 clock with no rendering, then the rest stats (pelvis / torso / head height, torso `up` = its local up's world y, settle time, max joint gap). The foe's transforms are restored after every trial. `shot` = a trial to render (camera on the corpse, `view` deg around it) and return as a JPEG.
- Runner `tests/ragdoll-lab.ts` (`[FOE=bandido] [N=40] [SECS=1.4] [WOUNDS=none,crouch,prone] [SHOTS=crouch:3,... ANGLE=90] npx tsx tests/ragdoll-lab.ts`, dev server on 5174): tutorial vs that outfit, DRAW, then the lab per wound; prints "seated" counts (torso up > 0.5 or head > 0.6m). Judge at SECS=1.4: the round's `cleanup()` freezes the corpse ~1.3-1.5s after the kill, so that is the pose players see.

### What was wrong (old ragdoll, N=40 per pose, at 1.4s)
- Standing deaths (most kills): EVERY outfit jackknifed: legs standing as straight columns, waist bent ~85 deg backwards, head on the dirt, hips 0.47-0.49m up (torso up -0.51), most still moving. Cause: symmetric cones. Knee 1.5 rad either way (hyperextends; the shared topple spin swung the shins back and the thighs forward = a reverse knee bend), waist 1.45 rad back, hips 1.6 rad back: the corpse locked into a bridge against those limits.
- Crouch deaths: seated 3/40 (outlaw), 8/40 (ghost), ~1 in 5-6 = the old "1 in 6": sitting on the flat pelvis box, the lunge's front knee up as a strut, torso upright. Prone: fine.
- Ghost: its duster's front panels are merged into the `Torso` mesh (can't skip by name), so its torso box was 0.21m longer, down past the pelvis: a pillar.

### Fix (`render/ragdoll.ts`)
- One-way joints: `JointSpec.bend` centres the cone `bend` rad off the parent limb (+ toward the character's back, - front), limb axis = local Y (down for legs, up for torso/head; both rigs authored that way, verified). Knee bend 1.2 / cone 1.25 (fold 0..2.45, no hyperextension); hips -0.8 / 1.15 (forward 1.95, back 0.35); waist -0.5 / 0.85 (forward 1.35, back 0.35); neck -0.2 / 0.7. A pose already past a range (prone knee 1.9, lunge back hip 1.2) widens that joint's cone to fit (+0.05) so no limit starts violated (that tears joints, 10-04 §11).
- `createRagdoll(..., facing)`: world facing at creation (standing), stored per body as the local "back" (`faceOf` in `home.ts`; the lab passes it too). Without it joints keep the old symmetric cones.
- Legs give out: an upright corpse's thighs swing the knees forward and the shins tip their tops forward (`LEG_BUCKLE` 3 rad/s) and the shins drop, while the trunk keeps the topple along the shot.
- Slump assist: for 2s after the hit, while the torso stands > ~60 deg upright (`SLUMP_UP` 0.5), up to 10 N*m on the torso alone tips it further the way it ALREADY leans (shot direction if none). Pushing along the shot instead: 40/40 crouch deaths stayed seated (the lunge's back leg kneels behind the pelvis like a tripod).
- Torso box clipped at the pelvis origin (torso-local y -0.45 on every rig): only the ghost changes.
- Tried and dropped: a round (sphere-capped) shin foot. The shin box is the boot (0.22m tall, 0.38m deep), so it never applied; the joints were the fix.

### Result (N=40 per pose and outfit, at 1.4s)
- Standing: pelvis 0.18-0.20m, torso flat (up ~0.03), lying on the back. Crouch: 0 seated on all four outfits; they fold forward onto the face (they were leaning that way) into a heap, hips ~0.43m. Prone unchanged. Max joint gap 0.011m.
- Left forearm: NOT added. In captures the left arm (short sleeve + cuff, one `Sleeve_L` mesh) lies along the torso and does not read stiff; at duel range it is a few pixels. A body would need a new elbow KEEP node in `build_glbs.py`, all six cowboy GLBs rebuilt and verified, and the procedural rig split.
