## 1. Hat = head, head priority (user: "i am hitting the hat constantly and its not counting it as a headshot")

- Causes: the 0.34m head sphere stopped at +0.34 above `headY`, under every
  crown (outlaw +0.49, top hat +0.72), and brim edges fell into the body
  capsule. Worse, the body capsule's top cap bulges to y 2.17 and ~0.5m in
  front of the face, and bullets are sampled every ~23cm, so 84% of
  dead-centre head shots and every chin shot scored as BODY hits.
- `testCapsuleHit(pos, feet, cap, hat?, dir?)` (projectiles.ts): upright
  poses check the head (r 0.25 = the visible head; collar and shoulder tops
  stay body) or the hat first. Once a sample enters the body, the line of
  fire is searched 1.3m ahead (2cm steps) for the head/hat. Prone keeps the
  old r 0.34 per-sample rule.
- Hat volume = a solid of revolution per 4cm band, measured off each GLB's
  `{P}_Hat` mesh at load (`cowboyGlb.ts measureDims`, sampled along triangle
  edges); `DEFAULT_HAT` (outlaw numbers) covers the procedural stand-in.
- Fire-time lethality (double-KO rule input) uses `predictHit()`: a
  scratch bullet through the live integrator + substep + hit test, replacing
  the "click within 90px of the head" guess.
- Foe aim moved from mid +0.12 to mid -0.07: with head priority the old
  scatter turned 0.3-2% foe headshots into 4-10% (sim); -0.07 keeps 2-4%,
  body hits unchanged.
- Verified: `tests/duel-headshot.spec.ts`, which covers chin = headshot,
  collar = body, crouched head = headshot, and for all 10 outfits a crown
  top + brim headshot plus a control shot past the brim (no hit). 11/11.

## 2. Crosshair cap (user: "limit the crosshair width to the enemy maximum width ... on that given range")

- `spreadCapDeg()` (home.ts) = the angle the foe's half-width subtends from
  the camera at the foe's capsule mid. The drawn ring (`crossPx`) AND the
  real spread (`sampleSpread(capDeg)`) clamp to it, so the ring never
  lies. Focus progress and the QTE needle speed keep the raw bloom (focusing
  still pays below the cap). Player only, as asked; the foe's own bloom is
  uncapped (open question for the user: cap it too? Yes, see §6).
- Half-width measured per GLB (standing, hat brim included): 0.46 for most
  rigs, bandido 0.63 (sombrero), widow 0.50, kate 0.49, ruby 0.48, ghost
  0.46. Cap: 2.26 deg at 6m, 1.36 at 15m, 0.91 at 25m. The unfocused bloom
  at DRAW (~2.7 deg) is ABOVE the cap at every range.
- Checked at 6/15/25m on 3 outfits: ring radius = foe half-width in px
  (59.5 vs 58.9 / 35.7 vs 35.5 / 23.8 vs 23.7), spreadCheck max 0.99.
- Specs: long random rolls keep the ring pinned at the cap, so ring-size
  assertions need a short range. "QTE hits ... gap down" and "leaving the
  holster restarts" now run at 8m (`__gamey.round(0, 8)`). "shot kicks
  x1.5" was passing by accident (ring at the cap before and after); it now
  runs the tutorial at 6m with 2 QTE hits first.
