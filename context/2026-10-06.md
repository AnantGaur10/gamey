## 2. Bloom fixes, AI bloom, QTE multipliers, street backdrop (later same day)
- **Player spread bug (since the initial commit):** `playerDirFromClick` offset the aim by world x/y x 1/40 of the bloom, so shots hugged the centre dot. Now the spread angle is applied across the screen (camera right/up) at the target's depth: 600 samples land at max 0.99 of the drawn radius, ~70% in the outer ring. The spread is sampled from the circle shown at the click, THEN the x1.5 kick (it used to kick first).
- **AI bloom (user: "enemy accuracy affected as much as the player"):** `foeAim` = a second `DuelMachine`, same gun numbers: focus result at DRAW from `focusQuality`, regrow until its first shot, x1.5 kick per shot, flinch 1.1 when hit / 0.9 when it lands a hit. Spread = bloom angle from the muzzle + angular hand wobble `0.03 x accuracyMult` (fitted by Monte Carlo so first-shot hit rates match the old per-tier values, e.g. tier 0/1/2 at 15m ~56/74/87% vs old 51/70/86%; kicked follow-ups land ~49/67/84%). User picked "retune to old hit rates" over a pure mirror (which hit ~99%: bloom angles are small vs the 0.62m body capsule).
- **Focus QTE (user):** good = bloom x0.5, perfect = x0.25 ("decrease by 0.75x" read as BY 75%, stronger than good), any miss (QTE miss or PC click) = x1.3; floor bloomMin, cap bloomMax. Guarded in `test:physics`.
- **Watchdog** now reads raw wall-clock frame time (clamped dt hid 300ms frames; the post pass stayed ~5s on software GL and broke the QTE-miss spec at 1920).
- **Street (user: too much sky / empty desert at long duels):** `build_street.py` `left_row()` adds a bank, assay office, barber (striped pole) and a set-back church with a steeple left of the livery; `backdrop()` adds three camera-facing ridge layers (near buttes + rock spires y -70, strata mesas y -120, dusty far range y -190), a windmill and a water tower. 24k -> 33.2k tris, ~1.0 -> 1.3MB, draw calls unchanged, two builds byte-identical. Fog hazes the ranges at noon, silhouettes them at dusk and hides them at night.
