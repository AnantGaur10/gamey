## 1. Cowboy look + low-end perf pass (user ask)
User: bake AO into the cowboys' vertex colours, rim light, ACES/AgX + per-time-of-day grade, secondary motion (hat/coat sway, dust kick on draw, camera shake on hits), FXAA ("MSAA is very expensive computationally use FXAA"), and merge the cowboy meshes (chosen over "AO bake only"). WebGPU / WASM rejected the same day: WebGPU still needs a WebGL2 fallback on the Chromebook/Safari targets and would mean rewriting every custom shader in TSL; the logic is far too small for WASM to pay off.

- **GLBs (`build_glbs.py`):** after the existing steps, modifiers applied, material base colour written per face into a `Col` corner attribute (metallic colours x (1 - 0.7 metallic): the old PBR metals rendered near-black with no env map, so the gun stays dark), every mesh joined into its nearest KEEP node (`Pelvis Torso Head Waist Sleeve_L Cuff_L Leg_L/R Knee_L/R Shin_L/R armR elbowR Gun GunHolster gunTip` + hat (`Top` + Brim/Band/Dent -> `{P}_Hat`) + `CoatTail_L/R`); empty KEEP nodes take their geometry on a direct child (`Sleeve_R`, `Fore_R` (keeps the ragdoll's /fore|hand/ filter), `Frame`). Cycles AO bake (distance 0.3m, ground plane at the feet), corner colour *= 1 - 0.6 (1 - ao); the gun arm is hidden while the body bakes (it swings up at DRAW), then baked in context. One material `M_Cowboy`; export with COLOR_0, no normals; `glb_util.slim_glb` now carries animation accessors. Result: 73 -> 15 meshes, 11 -> 1 material, ~297 -> 185KB each, tris 4348/4392 unchanged, every KEEP node's world TRS identical (0 diff), world vertex set identical (<0.1mm), same 28 clips.
- **Runtime:** GLB meshes get one shared flat `MeshLambertMaterial({vertexColors})` (old GLBs without COLOR_0 keep their colour). Rim = fresnel^3 added before fog (`addRimLight`, shared uniforms, `TimeOfDay.rim`), also on the procedural cowboy.
- **Post (`render/post.ts`):** renderer `antialias:false, alpha:false`; scene -> 8-bit LINEAR target -> one pass: three's FXAA (edge luma = sqrt(l/(1+l)) of the linear image) whose final sample is graded (lift/gain, saturation, ACES (AgX looked washed out next to the baked palette; `TONE_FN`), sRGB, contrast). Measured on SwiftShader 1920x1080 (fill-bound): old build ~183ms/frame; separate grade pass + half-float target ~267-300ms; sRGB 8-bit target ~300ms (sRGB decode on each of FXAA's ~20 taps); linear 8-bit ~200ms; no post 117ms. So `noteFrame` got a second stage: still slow (>22ms avg) at 1x -> `postOff` for the session, render direct (renderer ACES tone mapping in the materials; the sky shader includes the tone map/colorspace chunks for this path). Real GPUs keep FXAA + grade. Headless captures always end on the direct path. The CSS sky moved into the scene as a screen-space quad with the same stops (`TimeOfDay.sky`; `cssSkyForRound` now derives from it), so `__gamey.tod()` now also changes the sky. `renderer.info.autoReset=false`, reset per frame.
- Grades: noon exp 1.0, evening 0.95, night 1.4 (no lift: it would grey the fog and give the foe away), hell 1.1. Rim: noon 0.3, evening 0.3, night 0.1, hell 0.2.
- **Motion:** `render/sway.ts` damped springs on the hat (brim pivot) and coat tails (top pivot), driven by the parent's world acceleration + a little wind, stepped inside the fixed stepper, rendered by alpha, kicked on hits. Dust puff at both duelists' feet when DRAW starts. Camera shake on the player being hit (0.055m, head 0.09m) and a tiny one (0.012m) when the player lands a hit; applied just before the draw and restored after, so aim/projection/hit tests never see it.
- **Numbers (headless SwiftShader, DPR 1):** draw calls 157 -> 48 (hell 158 -> 56); ms/frame median 183 -> 133 at 1920x1080, 117 -> 67 at 1280x720 (direct path after the watchdog).
