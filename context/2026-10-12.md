Three subagents built the three sets in parallel on a shared kit; the outfits, runtime and integration were done in the main session.

### Maps (`src/render/maps.ts`)
- 4 maps: `street` (Main Street, unchanged), `saloon` (SALOON), `falls` (THE FALLS), `town` (TELEGRAPH ROW). Shown on the round line after the score (`· SALOON · 15 M`; Main Street keeps the old line).
- Choice: home-screen `MAP: <name> ▸` button cycles RANDOM / each map, saved in `Progress.settings.map` (optional string, validated with `isMapChoice`). Default RANDOM. A best-of-3 series stays on one map (rolled at series start, `SeriesState.map`); deathmatch rolls per duel (`DeathRun.map`, kept for its hell round); tutorial and single duels = Main Street. `rollMap` also preloads the set GLB.
- Time of day still follows the decided-round stage (noon / evening / night). Each map has its own colours (sky stops, fog colour, hemi sky/ground (new optional `TimeOfDay.hemiSky/hemiGround`), dir colour, grade, rim) but ALWAYS the base preset's `fogDensity` (`tint()` forces it), so night difficulty is identical on every map.
- Per map: arena floor look (`setFloor`: ground colour or hidden, ruts, sun disc; the arena plane drops to y -0.02 under a set's ground band), window-glow per stage, `S_Sky` window colour + `S_Shaft` beam strength per stage, wind-audio multiplier (saloon 0.2), ambient particle cap (no fireflies indoors), shadow-decal multiplier, `paintGain` (saloon 1.5: its lamp-light bake is dark), backdrop dust for misses past the foe (falls: ground).

### Hell versions

- Every new map's hell round = the SAME set re-tinted at runtime (`setGlb` `hellTint`: vertex colour luma -> very dark brown -> brown-red -> ember, 15% of the original kept; same ramp in `set_kit.hell_tint` for previews), hell light (shared `HELL_GRADE`, browner than the causeway's), red sky outdoors, embers, red glows; `S_Water` turns into glowing lava and drops its fog so the lava fall glows through the haze. ### Enemy outfits (`scripts/blender/outfits.py`, `npm run build:enemies`)
- `build_glbs.py` hook: `GLB_OUTFIT=<name>` re-dresses the OUTLAW rig after the standing pose / holster / idle solve and before the merge, and exports only `cowboy_<name>.glb`. Same `O_` nodes, clips, holster and hit capsules. Unset = the default build, verified byte-identical to the committed hero/outlaw.
- `bandido` (green poncho with white zig-zag + purple hem, thrown up over the gun hip so the holster and grip stay clear; straw sombrero with an upturned rim; crossed bandoliers; no coat tails) 4932 tris. `ghost` (white duster with knee-length tails + open front panels, wide flat white hat, green bandana mask, purple scarf, green vest) 4305. `dandy` (purple frock coat with long tails, green vest, white shirt front + cravat, tall top hat with a green band, white gloves, watch chain) 4305. All within the 3-5k budget, ~180-215KB.
- Who you meet (`src/render/foes.ts`): series stage 0 bandido, 1 ghost, 2 dandy (the AI tier follows the same stage, so a drawn round replays vs the same foe); deathmatch cycles outlaw, bandido, ghost, dandy; tutorial = outlaw; hell keeps the round's foe. The procedural fallback cowboy wears the outfit's colours.
- Ragdoll: a top hat made the head's collision box a 0.9m pole (sombrero: a 1.1m disc) and long tails stretched the torso box, so corpses settled propped up. `ragdoll.ts` `BODY_MESH_SKIP` sizes the head and torso bodies without `_Hat` / `CoatTail` subtrees (they still ride the parts). Applies to hero/outlaw too.
