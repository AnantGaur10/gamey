# 2026-11-02: UI sounds

User: "create ui sound effects when someone clicks or hovers over a button on the homepage or pause menu or anywhere". Music for the menu and the maps comes from the user later.

- Every `<button>` (all the game's clickables are buttons) gets a sound through one delegated listener pair in `src/ui/uiSfx.ts` (`installUiSfx(audio)`, called in `boot`). New screens get sounds for free. There is no pause menu (leaving the holster only pauses Focus); the in-duel buttons (SKIP, coach BACK/NEXT, result cards, revive) are covered.
- Sounds are Web Audio synth (`AudioManager.playUiSfx`, zero bytes) on the SFX bus, so the SFX slider, the player's mute, the site mute and ad muting all apply. Dry (no street echo).
  - hover: one cylinder notch, a tiny high tick. Mouse only (no touch hover), skips disabled buttons, at most one per 40ms.
  - click: the hammer cocking, two metal clicks 52ms apart.
  - confirm (`.primary`, `.go`: PLAY, NEXT, the result card's main button, name OK): cock + a short spur ring.
  - back (BACK, HOME, SKIP, revive NO): a duller cock.
- Hover never creates or resumes the AudioContext (no gesture yet = Chrome's "AudioContext was not allowed to start" warning), so the very first hover before any click is silent. Clicks may create/resume it.
- The click listener runs in CAPTURE, so a handler that swaps the screen can't drop the sound.
- Levels (speaker-output peak, SFX 100%): hover 0.077, back 0.08, click 0.15; the SETTINGS gunshot sample at 0.5 is 0.44.
- Spec: `tests/ui-sfx.spec.ts` (voices started per hover/click, none while muted, no AudioContext warnings).

## Recoil, metallic HUD, holster box fades (evening)

- **Recoil per gun** (user: "damage having the highest of the 3 then lifesteal then gold"): `GunDef.recoil` DAMAGE 1.0 / LIFESTEAL 0.75 / GOLD 0.5. It scales the shot's crosshair kick (`applyShotKick(recoil)`: 1 + 0.5 x recoil, so DAMAGE keeps the locked x1.5, LIFESTEAL x1.375, GOLD x1.25; fixed clock), the visible arm kick (0.42 x recoil rad) and roll, and a camera thump (`shakeCam(0.018 x recoil)`, render-only). RECOIL is a fourth stat bar on the armory cards (100 / 75 / 50).
- **Metallic HUD**: the cylinder is brushed steel with a silver rim and centre pin; live chambers are brass cartridge heads with a silver primer, spent ones dark steel bores (SVG gradients `m-steel/m-rim/m-brass/m-bore`; chambers are `<g class="chamber live|spent">`). QTE: silver track, gold zone, polished needle (`q-silver/q-gold/q-needle`, user-space gradients resized with the ring: a straight needle has a zero-width bbox).
- **Holster box fades while held** (user): Focus running = the mouse in the box / the thumb on it -> `.readyzone.qting` hides the text AND the dashed border; only the QTE remains. Leaving brings it back. Touch pad: no dashed border at all, its TAP caption hides while the QTE runs.
