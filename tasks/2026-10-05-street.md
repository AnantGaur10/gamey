## Where the progress lives
- **Source of truth:** `scripts/blender/build_street.py`. Re-running it rebuilds the whole set; no .blend is needed. Live: exec it through the socket (env `STREET_BAKE=0 STREET_EXPORT=0` for fast layout passes). Headless: `blender -b --factory-startup --python scripts/blender/build_street.py`.
- **Output:** `public/models/street.glb` (clean baked build).
- **Snapshot for viewing:** `blender/street_snapshot.blend` (saved 2026-10-05 12:29 from the live session: `Street` collection with 12 objects plus `GameCam`, a replica of the in-game camera at distM 11.5). View/inspect only. Edits made there do NOT reach the GLB; change the script instead. It carries the pre-fix baked colours (horse-neck blowout). Never touches `blender/cowboys.blend`.
- **Old street:** previous `street.glb` backed up at `~/.cache/gamey-street-work/street_old.glb`. The old Blender street is still in `blender/cowboys.blend` (`Street` collection).
- **Helper tools (outside the repo):** `~/.cache/gamey-street-work/`: `bl.py` (send a .py to the live socket), `render.py` (Workbench preview from GameCam), `shot.mjs <name> <WxH>` (in-game Playwright screenshot, needs the dev server on 5174), `glbstat.mjs` (tris/nodes/size).

Key finding: the locked ortho OTS camera (~5° pitch) only ever sees a ~12.5m × 3.6m strip of the facades (ground-floor storefronts up to the porch roofs) plus the ground in front. Sky never shows at 16:9. The old street put its signs/false fronts above that strip, so the frame was one flat brown wall. Detail now goes into vertical camera-facing surfaces below ~3.6m.
